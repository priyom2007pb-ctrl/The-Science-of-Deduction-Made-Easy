The Science of Deduction Made Easy

Welcome, everyone, to my website.

As you might already have guessed, this website is dedicated to "The Science of Deduction."

My name is Priyom Banerjee, and I'm an 18-year-old college student with an interest in observation, deduction, and the fascinating ways in which seemingly insignificant details can reveal much more than we expect.

Here, I'll teach you the scientific approach to observing, analysing, and deducing—inspired by the OG himself, Sherlock Holmes.

First, I'll break down the process of deduction step by step, showing you how to observe details, collect information, connect seemingly unrelated facts, and arrive at a logical conclusion.

Then comes the fun part.

I'll give you problems, puzzles, ciphers, and deduction challenges to put what you've learned into practice. Some will be straightforward. Others... perhaps not.
